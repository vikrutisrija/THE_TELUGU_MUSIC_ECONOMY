# The Telugu Music Economy

Growth Gaps, Missed Revenue, and What Labels Must Do Next

An independent research report exploring:

- Direct fan revenue opportunities
- Brand partnership gaps
- Creator economy opportunities
- Growth strategies for Telugu music labels

## Key Findings

- India crossed ₹6,000 crore music industry value
- Telugu labels are underutilizing audience assets
- Direct-to-fan monetization remains largely untapped
- Creator partnerships can become major growth channels
